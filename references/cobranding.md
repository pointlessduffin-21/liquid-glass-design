# Product and client branding

Use the product name in the app shell and page titles. This kit bundles no
company logos. Keep brand accents separate from status and chart colors.

For client deliverables, use an optional `.sb-client` sidebar slot or
`.auth-client` sign-in slot with an approved logo and accurate wording such as
“Built for” or “Proof of concept for”. Remove these slots when unused.

Obtain logos from the brand owner, preserve their proportions and colors,
and record the file source and download date in your project's brand folder.
Inspect downloaded SVGs for scripts, event handlers, external references, and
foreign objects before use. Do not imply endorsement or use real third-party
logos in sample data. Use generic placeholders in demos.
